# Dungeons

Dungeons are five-player instanced content — private copies for the group. They break the solo loop, gear a character faster than quest rewards, and teach the group skills raids demand later.

## Why Dungeons Matter

- **Gear:** bosses drop blues; end-of-dungeon quests award pieces that carry several levels.
- **The trinity in practice:** tank holds aggro, healer keeps everyone alive, damage dealers kill without pulling threat. Every dungeon run rehearses the raid format.
- **Social:** the players met in RFC and WC are the raid roster of December. Be pleasant, be punctual.

## Before the First Dungeon

1. Repair all gear.
2. Food and water for the class role; ammunition for Hunters.
3. Bag space for loot.
4. Know the basics of the role — tank, healer, or damage.
5. Find the dungeon quests. Most are offered near the entrance or in the zone hub.

## Ragefire Chasm: Quest Prep (Horde)

RFC has **no attunement and no key** — the portal in Orgrimmar's Cleft of Shadow is open from around level 10 and the run is tuned for 13–18. "Unlocking" it means collecting its five quests, all Horde-only and all available from level 9. Four are picked up before the run; the fifth starts inside.

| Quest | Giver | Where | Objective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Testing an Enemy's Strength | Rahauro | Thunder Bluff, Elder Rise | Kill 8 Ragefire Troggs and 8 Ragefire Shaman |
| Searching for the Lost Satchel | Rahauro | Thunder Bluff, Elder Rise | Find Maur Grimtotem's corpse in the first chamber, near Oggleflint; turn in inside, then return the satchel to Rahauro |
| The Power to Destroy... | Varimathras | Undercity, Royal Quarter | Loot Spells of Shadow and Incantations from the Nether from Searing Blade cultists |
| Slaying the Beast | Neeru Fireblade | Orgrimmar, Cleft of Shadow (beside the portal) | Kill Taragaman the Hungerer, bring his heart |
| Hidden Enemies | Thrall | Orgrimmar, Grommash Hold | Chain — see below |

**Hidden Enemies** is the chain that must be started early. Its first two steps happen outside the dungeon:

1. Kill Burning Blade cultists at Skull Rock, east of Orgrimmar, for a Lieutenant's Insignia.
2. Show the insignia to Neeru Fireblade, then report back to Thrall.
3. Now the dungeon step unlocks: kill Bazzalan and Jergosh the Invoker in RFC.
4. Speak with Neeru Fireblade again.
5. Return to Thrall for a choice of the Orgrimmar weapons (blue in Forever).

Do steps 1–2 before forming the group. A runner who arrives at RFC without them cannot pick up the dungeon step that run.

Efficient pickup loop: Elder Rise in Thunder Bluff (two quests) → zeppelin to Undercity (Varimathras) → Orgrimmar for Neeru and Thrall. Dungeon-quest XP in Forever is roughly double Classic values, so a fully loaded first RFC clear pays close to a full level around 13–14.

## First-Dungeon Rules

1. **Follow the tank.** The tank's pace is the group's pace.
2. **Let the tank pull.** Never open a fight before the tank — stray attacks pull packs the group did not plan for.
3. **Watch the healer's Mana.** Zero Mana means the group waits.
4. **Pull adds to the tank**, not away from it.
5. **Roll Need only on real upgrades.** Greed on sellables, Pass otherwise. Blue items the right choice is unclear on: ask the group before rolling.
6. **Loot chests last.** Pulls before treasure.
7. **Stay together.** Wandering solo is how dungeons wipe.
8. **Ask about skipped quests.** If the group skipped a quest giver, say so before the final boss.
9. **Wipes happen.** Run back together, no blame.
10. **Thank the group.** Reputation starts here.

## Meeting Stones and Travel

Dungeons have no queue system — travel is part of the price. Meeting stones near entrances allow summoning group members (two players channel to summon the rest). Flight points near dungeons are worth taking the long way around for once.
