# Endgame at 60

The class-neutral framework: what endgame is in Forever, what opens when, and the checklist that applies to every class. Class-specific endgame lives in the class guides — see the [Orc Hunter endgame](/docs/classes/hunter/orc-endgame) for a worked example.

## The Shape of Endgame in Forever

- The level cap is **60, permanently**. Progression is horizontal: gear, the account-wide **Legacy system** (Adventure, Professions, and Resourcefulness trees at launch), professions, reputation, PvP ranks.
- Raids did **not** open at launch. The first three raids unlock together on **December 9, 2026**, five weeks after launch — a deliberate runway to reach 60 and prepare.
- Confirmed first tier, from [Blizzard's raid overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303009/world-of-warcraft-forever-raid-overviewlocations-raid-sizes-and-attunement):

| Raid | Size | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barrow Deeps | 10 players | New |
| Hyjal Summit | 20 players | New |
| Onyxia's Lair | 40 players | Classic-era raid |

- **Molten Core and Blackwing Lair are not announced for Forever.** The summer 2027 roadmap slots a "revamped iconic raid" without a name, and Blizzard has said older raids such as Molten Core would return with new mechanics. Classic-era boss notes remain useful reference, but do not plan around MC/BWL for the first tier.

## The Four Pillars of 60

1. **Raids.** Barrow Deeps (10), Hyjal Summit (20), Onyxia's Lair (40) from December 9. Guilds are the vehicle.
2. **Attunements.** Quest chains that unlock raid entrances — start them before 60 where possible. See below.
3. **Pre-raid gear.** Level 60 dungeons: Blackrock Depths, Lower/Upper Blackrock Spire, Stratholme, Scholomance, Dire Maul, plus the new high-level dungeons (Alcaz Prison, Shaper's Terrace, Krol'dok Stronghold).
4. **Economy and PvP.** The level-60 money engine (professions, farming, the auction house) and the battleground ladder.

## Week One at 60

- [ ] Train level-60 abilities and finish any outstanding trainer visits.
- [ ] Finish zone quest hubs in the 55–60 bands (Eastern Plaguelands, Silithus, Winterspring) — the gold adds up.
- [ ] Run the 56–60 dungeons for gear and attunement progress.
- [ ] Start raid attunements (below).
- [ ] Max First Aid; stock bandages, food, and class consumables.
- [ ] Join a guild running the raid sizes that fit. Ask about loot rules before committing.

## Attunements

### Onyxia's Lair

Confirmed by Blizzard; the chain is long and runs through Blackrock Depths and Upper Blackrock Spire. The Horde-side chain (classic-derived, structure unverified on live): a quest from Kargath in the Badlands ("Warlord's Command"), a Rend Blackhand kill in UBRS, then the Emberstrife chain through Dustwallow and the Dragonmaw clan tests. Start at 58–59; do not leave it for raid night.

### Upper Blackrock Spire key — Seal of Ascension

The classic key chain runs through LBRS with gems from bosses and an egg from Dustwallow. UBRS hosts Onyxia attunement steps, so the key is effectively mandatory. **Classic-derived — verify on live.**

### Molten Core / Blackwing Lair

Not announced. If a revamped Molten Core arrives (summer 2027 window), expect an attunement similar to the classic "Attunement to the Core" portal chain. Treat everything here as provisional.

## The Pre-Raid Gear Circuit

Forever's tuning keeps dungeon and quest gear competitive, so the circuit is: **quest blues from 55–60 hubs → dungeon blues from the 56–60 dungeons → crafted epics where professions allow**. Do not chase auction-house epics; the first raid tier arrives within weeks of any fresh 60.

Class guides carry the specific circuit tables.

## Beyond the First Tier

Blizzard's roadmap: spring 2027 adds two raids (10 and 20 players), a legendary questline, two dungeons, and a new area; summer 2027 adds the revamped iconic raid plus one more new raid. The level-60 gear economy is designed so older raid items stay useful — chase drops exist so old raids remain worth running.
