# Windshaper Skyborne Shaman: Levels 1–60

This is a complete Horde path from Zephras Isle to level 60. It preserves the supplied guide's act-based route, checklists, class lessons, and gear checks while distinguishing current facts from beta observations and recommendations.

For shared interface, travel, profession, and grouping basics, read the [Horde New Player Guide](/docs/new-player/horde).

## Evidence and How to Use This Guide

- **Current/confirmed** describes access, faction, and class systems supported by current references.
- **Beta-reported** identifies quest names, route details, exact tuning, vendor locations, and other observations that may have changed.
- **Guide recommendation** marks a route, specialization, equipment, profession, or efficiency choice rather than a game rule.

Play the act matching your level. If the current game, quest log, or ability tooltip disagrees with the guide, trust the game and update the guide.

## Act 0: Account, Faction, and Character Setup

Windshaper Skyborne require the **Skyborne Heroic Pack or a higher eligible upgrade**. The pack does not replace the active World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time required to play Forever.

- Confirm the account has both the required game access and Skyborne unlock before planning a character.
- Choose **Skyborne**, then the **Windshaper** branch for **Horde**.
- Choose **Shaman** as your class.
- Coordinate faction and Ruleset with friends before character creation. Your Skyborne faction choice determines who you can join and part of your racial kit.
- Plan to remain on Zephras Isle for its opening quest path before meeting mainland friends around level 12–13.

**Lesson:** Access, faction, and Ruleset are account/character setup decisions. Settle them before anyone creates a character.

## Act 1: Thendal — Levels 1–4

The opening isle quests introduce movement, vendors, and Shaman combat. Use Lightning Bolt to begin at range, then continue with melee and available abilities when an enemy closes.

- Accept the opening quests around **Thendal** and follow their objectives.
- Pull one enemy at a time and watch your **Mana**. Frequent casting can force recovery breaks.
- Loot useful items and sell unwanted gray items at the hub vendor.
- Review your weapon and bag space before leaving for another quest loop.
- Visit the Shaman trainer as early abilities become available.
- Use food, drink, or healing tools appropriate to your current character and situation.

:::note Beta-reported route details
The source document names early quests such as Coming of Age, Harmony in Balance, and Elemental Unrest. Current quest names and objectives take priority over those beta captures.
:::

**Lesson:** Lightning Bolt at range, melee when necessary, and Mana pacing are the opening Shaman rhythm.

**Gear check:** Use the best suitable staff, mace, axe, or other available weapon. Weapon skill and usable upgrades matter more than collecting minor armor purchases.

## Act 2: Shen'dar Village and the Highlands — Levels 4–7

Follow the opening quest path toward **Shen'dar Village**. This stretch introduces Earth totems and more of the Skyborne movement toolkit.

- Complete the Shaman's Earth totem quest as it becomes available around level 4.
- Review Earth totem choices such as Stoneskin or Strength of Earth and use the one appropriate to your current fight.
- Learn and practice **Walk on Air**, the Skyborne glide, in a safe location before relying on it for a dangerous drop.
- Visit trainers when your quest loop brings you back to a hub.
- Use campsites and their shared benefits when available, and log out in rested areas.
- Keep water or other Mana recovery supplies if repeated casting is slowing you down.

:::note Beta-reported route details
The source names Reading the Ley Lines, Anchors of Zephras, Falling With Style, and The Great Outdoors as beta quest references. Treat those names and their exact placement as changeable.
:::

**Lesson:** Totems provide persistent area utility. Place them where they will remain useful rather than constantly replacing them between small movements.

**Gear check:** Maintain a usable weapon and read weapon-training options before equipping an unfamiliar type.

## Act 3: Valanaar — Levels 7–10

Continue toward **Valanaar**, the coastal hub and departure area in the supplied isle route. Its denser quests provide more opportunities to practice combat and recovery.

- Set a useful Hearthstone location while completing quests around the hub.
- Pull coastal enemies one at a time rather than gathering groups without the tools to control them.
- Complete the Fire totem class quest as it becomes available around level 10.
- Review **Flame Shock**, **Flametongue Weapon**, and Fire totem options as they enter your toolkit.
- Use healing between fights and watch Mana before starting another pull.
- Finish class and departure quests before leaving the isle.

:::note Beta-reported route details
Crab Season, Blood Tithe, Make Yourself Useful, and Call of Fire are named in the source's beta route. Follow current quest objectives if they differ.
:::

**Lesson:** Earth and Fire totems serve different jobs. Combine defensive or utility tools with damage rather than assuming every totem belongs in every fight.

**Gear check:** Equip useful quest weapon upgrades and keep supplies and free bag space for the mainland transition.

## Act 4: Leaving Zephras Isle — Levels 10–12

Finish the remaining Valanaar and isle quests before departure. The isle is self-contained, so check for completed turn-ins and class quests before leaving.

- Review your quest log for unfinished business you intentionally want to complete.
- Read the departure destination before boarding a skyboat.
- Keep your weapon, totems, healing tools, and recovery supplies ready for the mainland.
- Use the guided mainland quest chain rather than skipping directly to an unfamiliar city without direction.

:::warning Beta-reported travel detail
The source document instructs Windshapers to take the **western skyboat** toward the Thunder Bluff area and warns that another route leads to Horde-hostile Dalaran. Verify the current destination labels and safety before boarding; do not treat beta dock behavior as a current guarantee.
:::

**Lesson:** Transportation labels matter. Check where a vessel goes before committing to the trip.

## Act 5: Mainland Handoff — Levels 12–13

The supplied route brings you through the Thunder Bluff area and then toward Orgrimmar as part of the guided Horde transition.

- Follow the current Welcome to Azeroth or equivalent introductory chain.
- Visit Shaman trainers and weapon masters as the route reaches Horde cities.
- Train two-handed weapon types you intend to use; weapon skill grows through use after training.
- Review bank, auction house, and repair services.
- Meet friends in Orgrimmar when your levels and quests align.
- Set your Hearthstone at the hub you will actually use for the next quest band.
- Follow the quest direction toward the Barrens.

**Lesson:** The mainland handoff supplies trainers, services, and your first natural Horde meetup. Prepare once rather than making separate trips for each forgotten task.

**Gear check:** Train relevant weapon skills and consider additional bag space before beginning longer Barrens loops.

## Act 6: The Barrens — Levels 13–16

Use the Crossroads as your main Barrens hub. Take its flight point and collect a manageable set of quests for each directional loop.

- Batch nearby objectives instead of leaving town for one quest at a time.
- Prepare for **Ragefire Chasm** when your group reaches its level band.
- Collect dungeon quests before entering and agree on group roles.
- Use **Enhancement** if you want the guide's recommended melee-oriented solo path.
- Start or continue Cooking and other secondary professions that support your journey.
- Begin saving for later training, equipment, and mount expenses.

**Guide recommendation:** Enhancement combines melee, shocks, totems, and self-healing for an accessible solo path. Elemental and Restoration remain valid choices when you prefer ranged casting or group healing, but they change your Mana and equipment needs.

**Lesson:** Quest batching and role-aware preparation save more time than repeatedly clearing a dungeon without its quests.

**Gear check:** A suitable two-handed weapon is the guide recommendation for this Enhancement path. Keep a weapon imbue active when you have learned one.

## Act 7: Ruins of Lordaeron — Levels 16–20

Continue Barrens questing and consider **Ruins of Lordaeron** as a prepared Horde dungeon run during its level band. Wailing Caverns is another optional group destination when quests and friends make it convenient.

- Collect available dungeon quests before entering.
- Review your current role: melee damage, ranged damage, or healing support.
- Do not replace the tank's threat plan with untested abilities or totems.
- Keep healing and Mana tools available even when not acting as the group's main healer.
- At level 20, review **Call of the Elements**, **Totemic Recall**, and **Ghost Wolf**.
- Configure a totem preset only with totems you have actually unlocked.
- Train weapon skills and maintain your current weapon imbue.

**Current class system:** Call of the Elements places a configured totem group with one cast, Totemic Recall returns part of active totem Mana cost when recalled, and Ghost Wolf improves travel. Totemic Projection enters the toolkit at level 22 in the supplied current route.

**Gear check:** Use leather armor until mail becomes available at 40. Do not equip an early-mail recommendation from an older draft; current Shaman leveling references use leather-to-mail progression.

## Act 8: Branching Out — Levels 20–30

The 20s offer several Horde routes: southern Barrens, Ashenvale, Hillsbrad, and Thousand Needles among them. Choose based on level, quest density, and your current group.

- Use Camp Taurajo, Splintertree Post, Tarren Mill, Freewind Post, or other suitable hubs as your route requires.
- Complete the Water totem class progression around level 20.
- Learn **Totemic Projection** at level 22 and reposition active totems when combat moves away from them.
- Review Air totem progression as you approach its later unlock.
- Practice dungeon damage or healing with equipment suitable for that role.
- Choose professions that support your goals rather than copying a pairing you will not use.

**Guide recommendation:** Skinning, Mining, or Herbalism can support income; Engineering pairs with gadgets; Alchemy supports consumable-focused play. These are choices, not mandatory class pairings.

**Lesson:** Totem management now includes placement, recall, and repositioning. Moving efficiently includes moving your area utility when it matters.

## Act 9: The 30s, Riverglades, and Level 40 — Levels 30–45

Use suitable 30s quest bands such as Hillsbrad, Arathi, or the Shimmering Flats before entering Riverglades during its intended level range.

- Take flight points as you enter new hubs.
- Follow Riverglades' main quest arc rather than treating it as a single detour.
- Prepare for **Krol'dok Stronghold** with its quests at the appropriate level.
- At level 40, review mail armor training and equip suitable mail upgrades.
- Review your racial mount options and purchase or use the current mount you can afford and prefer.
- Continue Enhancement if you enjoy its melee path, or reassess Elemental/Restoration based on actual play.
- Maintain weapon imbues and totem presets as your toolkit expands.

:::note Beta-reported mount details
The source document names Galestrider racial mounts at 40 and 60, but exact vendors and prices were beta details. Check the current Skyborne vendor, riding requirements, and cost rather than relying on an estimated location.
:::

**Guide recommendation:** Windfury Weapon and a slow two-handed weapon form a familiar Enhancement recommendation. Current talent and weapon tooltips should guide the final choice.

**Lesson:** Level 40 improves travel and armor. Riverglades and Krol'dok provide a structured mid-level chapter rather than a mandatory speedrun route.

## Act 10: The Road to 60 — Levels 45–60

Use appropriate later zones and selected dungeons to complete the journey. The source route offers Felwood, Tanaris, the Hinterlands, Un'Goro, Azshara, and the Blasted Lands as options across different level bands.

- Choose zones appropriate to your current level instead of following a fixed zone after its quests have become trivial.
- Consider Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, and Shaper's Terrace at their listed dungeon bands.
- Prepare dungeon quests and role-specific equipment before each run.
- At 60, review current racial mount options and endgame class requirements.
- Choose Enhancement, Elemental, or Restoration based on the role you want to perform.
- Treat Mount Hyjal as endgame content rather than a place to begin leveling at 58.

:::note Beta-reported route details
Exact zone/dungeon sequencing and level-60 mount references retain the source document's route recommendations and beta observations. Current availability and tooltips take priority.
:::

**Lesson:** Arrive at 60 with a chosen role, suitable gear, and a group plan. Do not wait until the final level to learn the job you want in endgame groups.

## Shaman Leveling Systems

### Totems

Earth totems enter early, Fire follows around 10, Water around 20, and Air later in the supplied progression. Each school offers different utility; there is no single best preset for every fight.

**Call of the Elements** places a configured group of totems. **Totemic Recall** helps recover Mana when you finish with active totems. **Totemic Projection** repositions them as combat moves. Read current tooltips for costs, ranges, and limits.

### Weapon Imbues

Weapon imbues provide persistent enhancements. Flametongue is an early option; Windfury is a common later Enhancement recommendation. Use an imbue appropriate to your weapon, role, and current abilities rather than assuming one is universal.

### Armor and Weapons

Use leather until 40, then suitable mail armor. Train required weapon types at Horde weapon masters and raise weapon skill through use. A two-handed weapon fits the guide's Enhancement recommendation; one-handed weapon and shield setups serve other situations and roles.

### Three Shaman Directions

- **Enhancement:** Melee-oriented solo and group damage with weapons, shocks, and totems.
- **Elemental:** Ranged spellcasting with different Mana and equipment priorities.
- **Restoration:** Group healing and support, especially when you intend to fill that role in dungeons.

These are playstyle choices. Trying another role requires appropriate abilities, equipment, and group agreement—not merely changing a title.

### Windshaper Racials

Current references describe:

- **Walk on Air:** Controlled gliding.
- **Skysight:** Windshaper movement utility.
- **Wind Blessed:** Passive Haste.
- **Elemental Insight:** Increased damage against Elementals.

Exact percentages, durations, and cooldowns have changed during beta. Use current tooltips rather than memorized beta tuning.

### Professions and Mounts

Gathering professions support income; crafting professions support equipment, consumables, or gadgets. Choose based on what you will actually use. Cooking and other secondary professions support regular play and campsite preparation.

Plan for mount and riding costs using current prices. The source's Galestrider names and vendor locations are beta details, not spending instructions to follow blindly.

### Dungeon Roles

Agree on your role before the run. Bring role-suitable equipment, watch the healer's Mana, follow the tank's approach, and place totems where they help rather than pulling extra enemies. Prepare quests before entering, then decide whether another run serves a gear or social goal.

## Speed and Efficiency Rules

- [ ] Batch quests by hub and direction.
- [ ] Prepare dungeon quests before entering.
- [ ] Use rested areas and useful campsite benefits.
- [ ] Take flight points as you reach hubs.
- [ ] Keep weapon skills, weapon upgrades, and imbues current.
- [ ] Watch Mana; allow recovery rather than forcing constant casting.
- [ ] Use Skysight or other movement tools according to current tooltips.
- [ ] Reposition or recall totems when leaving their useful area.
- [ ] Pull one enemy at a time while learning; area tools do not guarantee control of a large pack.
- [ ] Save for meaningful training, travel, and mount expenses.
