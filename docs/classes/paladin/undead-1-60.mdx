# Undead Paladin: Levels 1–60

This is a complete Horde path from Deathknell to level 60. It follows level-ordered acts with practical checklists, class lessons, and gear checks. It is written for learning the class, not setting a speed record.

If you are new to the game, read the [Horde New Player Guide](/docs/new-player/horde) for shared onboarding. This page focuses on the Undead Paladin journey.

## Evidence and How to Use This Guide

- **Current/confirmed** describes access and class systems supported by current references or the source document's confirmed design ledger.
- **Beta-reported** identifies quest names, exact tuning, locations, vendor details, and other observations that can change.
- **Guide recommendation** marks a route, role, gear, profession, or efficiency choice rather than a game rule.

Play the act matching your current level. If the current game, quest log, or ability tooltip disagrees with this guide, trust the game and update the guide.

## Act 0: Setup and Meeting Friends

Undead Paladin requires the normal Forever subscription or Game Time, not a separate race unlock. Coordinate **Horde** and the same **Ruleset** with friends before creating characters.

- [ ] Choose **Undead** and **Paladin** at character creation.
- [ ] Expect to spend the opening levels in Tirisfal while Orc and Troll friends begin in Durotar.
- [ ] Do not travel across the world during the first few levels just to meet. Progress your own starting zone first.
- [ ] Use the Undercity–Orgrimmar zeppelin for a later dungeon meetup rather than changing your Hearthstone away from your quest hub.
- [ ] Pick a name you will still enjoy after the journey to 60.

**Lesson:** The opening zones are far apart, but Horde travel gives you a straightforward way to meet once your quests and level make it worthwhile.

## Act 1: Deathknell — Levels 1–4

Your early Paladin combat combines a Seal, Judgement, automatic melee attacks, and recovery between fights.

- [ ] Accept the shared Deathknell quests and the Paladin introduction chain.
- [ ] Keep **Seal of Righteousness** active when it is your chosen damage Seal.
- [ ] Learn **Judgement** at level 4. In Forever, using Judgement does not consume your active Seal.
- [ ] Learn **Blessing of Might** and maintain the long-duration Blessing. Recast it after death or after using another Blessing that replaces it.
- [ ] Pull one enemy at a time, loot everything useful, and sell unwanted gray items.
- [ ] Favor a suitable two-handed weapon and bag space over buying minor armor upgrades.

:::note Beta-reported route and trainer details
The source document names the Paladin introductory quests as Rediscovering the Light, A Difficult Path, and Coming to Terms. Trainer levels in this act, including Judgement at 4, come from its beta ability ledger. Follow the current class chain and trainer if they differ.
:::

**Lesson:** Seal active, Judgement available, automatic attacks between abilities, and recover before the next pull.

- [ ] **Gear check:** Equip the best usable two-handed weapon from your quests or drops. Keep bags free for the next loop.

## Act 2: Brill and the Road — Levels 4–7

Follow the quest path to Brill and continue the class training. At level 6, **Holy Strike** gives you an active melee damage button alongside Judgement.

- [ ] Continue the Paladin class chain when it directs you onward.
- [ ] Train Holy Strike and use it when it contributes meaningfully to a fight.
- [ ] Learn **Divine Protection** as an emergency defensive tool.
- [ ] Work through Brill-area quests in loops rather than repeated single-objective trips.
- [ ] Keep quest items until their named turn-in, and read vendor purchases before buying ordinary trade goods.
- [ ] Follow the class storyline toward **Bandarion Keep** in northwest Tirisfal.

:::note Beta-reported route and trainer details
Quest items such as Coarse Thread and specific Brill-area turn-ins come from the source document's beta route. Divine Protection's listed level and other trainer details should be checked against the current trainer. Holy Strike at 6 is supported by the current class reference.
:::

**Lesson:** Holy Strike makes early Paladin combat more active. Use your abilities deliberately rather than waiting only for automatic attacks.

- [ ] **Gear check:** Maintain your two-handed weapon, water or recovery supplies, and enough bag space for class quest items.

## Act 3: Bandarion Keep — Levels 7–10

Bandarion Keep at Tyr's Watch serves as the Forsaken Paladin class hub in the supplied Forever route. Treat its class quests as part of your character's progression, not just optional story.

- [ ] Complete the keep's introductory and class quests as they become available.
- [ ] Pull camp enemies from the edges rather than rushing into linked groups.
- [ ] Learn **Hammer of Justice** at level 8 and keep its stun available for dangerous moments.
- [ ] At level 10, learn **Seal of Fury**. Its Judgement-based taunt gives Paladins a tanking and rescue tool.
- [ ] At level 10, learn **Righteous Fury** when preparing to tank or intentionally hold threat.
- [ ] Learn **Blessing of Protection** and understand that using one Blessing can replace another; recast your chosen long-term Blessing afterward.
- [ ] Train relevant one- and two-handed weapon skills when you visit appropriate city trainers.
- [ ] Prepare for the resurrection lesson and **Redemption** as the class chain continues.

:::note Beta-reported route and trainer details
The source names Murlocs at the Gates, Touring the Grounds, The Tarnished, A Token of Good Faith, and A Lesson in Divinity. Quest order, item requirements, and NPC routing may differ. Hammer of Justice and Blessing of Protection levels come from the beta ledger. Seal of Fury and Righteous Fury at 10 are supported by the current Paladin reference.
:::

**Lesson:** The keep is a class home, and Paladins can protect others through more than healing. Taunting, threat management, immunity, and later resurrection all matter.

- [ ] **Gear check:** Use a suitable class or quest weapon and keep enough Linen or other requested supplies only when the current quest asks for them.

## Act 4: Finishing Tirisfal — Levels 10–12

Complete the remaining Tirisfal quest arcs before moving south. This is also the time to learn Undercity's services and transportation.

- [ ] Finish nearby Agamand Mills and remaining Scarlet or village quests in grouped loops.
- [ ] Defer optional fights that are consistently defeating you; return with more levels or a group.
- [ ] Learn **Redemption** at level 12. You can resurrect other players after combat or during recovery.
- [ ] Visit Undercity for trainers, bank, auction house, and weapon skills.
- [ ] Learn the zeppelin towers outside Undercity and verify destinations before boarding.
- [ ] Carry a delivery or next-zone quest toward Silverpine when your current quest path offers it.

:::note Beta-reported route and trainer details
Redemption at 12 and the named Tirisfal route details come from the source document's beta ledger. Check the current class chain and trainer for the live requirement.
:::

**Lesson:** Tirisfal teaches single-pull discipline, Blessing management, and class responsibilities. Silverpine expands those habits into longer quest chains.

- [ ] **Gear check:** Train weapon types you actually expect to use and raise their skill through combat. A new weapon type with no skill will miss often.

## Act 5: Silverpine Forest — Levels 12–16

Use the Sepulcher as your Silverpine hub. Accept a manageable set of quests, complete them by area, and return to turn in batches.

- [ ] Take the Sepulcher flight point and review its vendors and quest givers.
- [ ] Watch dangerous patrols and avoid elite or opposing-faction areas unless your group is prepared.
- [ ] Learn **Blessing of Wisdom** at level 14. It is an option when Mana recovery matters more than Might's offensive benefit.
- [ ] Learn **Retribution Aura** at level 16.
- [ ] Use the Righteous Fury learned at level 10 when you intentionally want threat, especially in a tanking role—not as an automatic solo damage setting.
- [ ] Keep chained quest items until their current objective is complete.

:::note Beta-reported route and trainer details
Named Sepulcher, ferry, southern Silverpine, and patrol instructions in the source route are beta observations. Blessing of Wisdom at 14 and Retribution Aura at 16 also come from its beta ability ledger. Check current trainers and objectives.
:::

**Lesson:** Blessing of Wisdom versus Might is a situational choice. Aura and threat management become increasingly important when you join groups.

- [ ] **Gear check:** Continue using suitable quest equipment. Paladins use mail before plate becomes available at 40; do not buy every armor upgrade.

## Act 6: Ruins of Lordaeron — Levels 15–20

The supplied Forever route places **Ruins of Lordaeron** above Undercity as a level 15–20 dungeon with strong Horde story and quest value.

### Prepare for the dungeon

- [ ] Collect available dungeon quests before entering.
- [ ] Repair, clear bag space, and bring recovery supplies.
- [ ] Decide whether you are joining as damage, tank, or healer and bring equipment appropriate to that job.
- [ ] Tanking uses intentional threat tools such as Seal of Fury and Righteous Fury. Do not enable a tank setup while playing ordinary group damage.
- [ ] Ask about loot before rolling Need.

At level 20, review **Consecration**, **Blessing of Kings**, and **Exorcism**. Consecration is a Mana-expensive area tool; Blessing of Kings is a long-duration group buff; Exorcism is used against appropriate enemy types.

:::note Beta-reported trainer details
The level-20 Consecration, Blessing of Kings, and Exorcism milestones come from the source document's beta ledger. Verify their current trainer levels and effects.
:::

**Guide recommendation:** Retribution remains a straightforward solo direction, while dungeon runs are a good place to try tanking or healing deliberately. Seal of Command and slow two-handed weapons are a source-guide recommendation; verify the current talent tree before building around a specific interaction.

**Lesson:** A quest-prepared first dungeon clear teaches group roles and provides useful rewards. Repeated clears are optional rather than the default leveling engine.

- [ ] **Gear check:** Equip useful dungeon upgrades and keep a slow, suitable two-handed weapon if following the Retribution recommendation.

## Act 7: The 20s — Levels 20–30

You have two practical Horde paths: Hillsbrad around Tarren Mill or the Barrens with friends. Travel options make it reasonable to combine them when quests and groups call for it.

- [ ] Use Tarren Mill for Hillsbrad quest bands and the Crossroads for Barrens quest bands.
- [ ] Run Wailing Caverns once with its quests if your group is nearby and ready.
- [ ] Continue Retribution for a simple solo base, or practice Protection/Holy in groups with the correct equipment.
- [ ] Keep weapon skills current through regular use and training.
- [ ] Maintain Mana discipline: use expensive area abilities for appropriate packs, not every weak single target.
- [ ] Save money for later training, equipment, and travel even though the class mount route reduces one major expense.

**Guide recommendation:** Carry role-appropriate secondary equipment if you intend to heal or tank. A two-handed damage set does not prepare you for either job by itself.

**Lesson:** The 20s are about choosing useful hubs and learning which Paladin role you actually enjoy.

## Act 8: The 30s — Levels 30–39

Use Arathi Highlands, the Shimmering Flats, Thousand Needles, or other suitable Horde quest bands. Choose routes based on quest density, current level, and available groups rather than trying to complete every zone.

- [ ] Take flight points at hubs such as Hammerfall and Freewind Post.
- [ ] Use a current two-handed weapon for the Retribution-oriented route.
- [ ] Continue First Aid and other secondary professions as useful recovery and support tools.
- [ ] Practice dungeon roles only with the gear, talents, and group agreement they require.
- [ ] Keep materials that support your professions or current quests, and sell what you do not need.
- [ ] Consider **Excavation Site: Wetlands** only when a prepared group is already nearby and its quests justify the Alliance-adjacent detour.

**Guide recommendation:** The source document's exact Retribution talent order predates later tree adjustments. Use the current talent tooltips and linked current Paladin reference rather than following an older point sequence blindly.

**Lesson:** Weapon upgrades, Mana awareness, and travel planning matter more than collecting every available quest.

## Act 9: Riverglades and the Warhorse — Levels 40–45

At level 40, review plate armor training and the Paladin **Summon Warhorse** class milestone. The supplied guide identifies the warhorse as a class mount that avoids the ordinary mount purchase; verify the current trainer and quest requirements before spending around that assumption.

- [ ] Enter Riverglades during its appropriate mid-30s-to-mid-40s band.
- [ ] Follow its main quest arc and use your new travel and armor capabilities.
- [ ] Prepare for **Krol'dok Stronghold** with its quests when your group reaches the dungeon band.
- [ ] Use **The Drowned City** as an optional 35–40 dungeon alternative when its level and quests fit your group.
- [ ] Reassess whether Retribution, Protection, or Holy is the role you want to pursue at 60.
- [ ] Spend savings deliberately on useful weapons, bags, training, and profession needs.

**Guide recommendation:** Use Riverglades as the default mid-level path and Krol'dok as a quest-prepared dungeon chapter. Alternative zones remain valid when your group or level calls for them.

**Lesson:** Level 40 changes travel and survivability. It is also the right time to choose an endgame direction based on experience rather than guesswork.

## Act 10: The Road to 60 — Levels 45–60

The final stretch combines Horde quest zones with selected dungeon runs.

- [ ] Use the Plaguelands and other appropriate 45–60 zones for your current level and role.
- [ ] Exorcism-focused Paladin play benefits when quests and dungeons contain suitable enemies.
- [ ] Consider Scholomance and Stratholme when their undead encounters, quests, and equipment suit your group.
- [ ] Consider the source route's newer dungeon ladder: Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, and Shaper's Terrace at their appropriate bands.
- [ ] Collect dungeon quests before a run and treat repeated farming as a separate goal.
- [ ] At 60, review the Forsaken Paladin class-mount quest at Bandarion Keep and its current requirements.
- [ ] Choose an endgame role and build equipment around that role.
- [ ] Check current raid schedules and preparation requirements. The source document lists Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, and Onyxia's Lair as scheduled to open December 9, 2026.

:::note Beta-reported route details
Exact dungeon order, class-mount quest details, and zone recommendations in this act combine the source's beta route with guide recommendations. Current game levels, quests, and availability take priority.
:::

**Lesson:** Reach 60 with a chosen role, appropriate gear, and a group plan. Leveling ending is a transition, not the end of progression.

## Level-60 Character Audit

- [ ] Choose a primary role and understand its core talents and equipment.
- [ ] Raise the weapon skills needed for that role, including one-handed weapon and shield for tanking when relevant.
- [ ] Understand your available Seals, Blessings, Auras, and threat settings.
- [ ] Practice Mana control rather than relying on constant recovery breaks.
- [ ] Review the warhorse and epic class-mount requirements at their current milestones.
- [ ] Maintain useful secondary professions and role-appropriate supplies.
- [ ] Keep repair, training, and respec funds available.
- [ ] Find a guild or steady group whose schedule and expectations fit you.

## Paladin Leveling Systems

### Seals and Judgement

Judgement no longer consumes your active Seal. Keep your chosen Seal active and use Judgement without rebuilding the Seal after every cast. Different Seals serve damage, tanking, recovery, or situational purposes; read their current tooltips.

### Holy Strike

Holy Strike is a baseline early active melee ability in Forever, with level 6 given by current class references. It gives early combat another meaningful button and interacts with later talents.

### Blessings and Auras

Blessings have long durations in Forever. Recast your intended Blessing after death or after an emergency Blessing replaces it. Blessing of Kings is part of the early level-20 toolkit in the supplied route.

Auras are persistent, exclusive choices. Use the aura appropriate to your role and current content rather than leaving one active out of habit.

### Mana Economy

Consecration and frequent ability use can drain Mana quickly. Use area abilities when the number of enemies justifies them, recover between pulls, and consider Blessing of Wisdom when sustain matters more than offense.

### Undead Racials

The supplied guide emphasizes Cannibalize as a between-pull recovery tool for health and Mana, Will of the Forsaken as a reactive effect break, Touch of the Grave as sustain, and extended underwater breathing. Exact percentages and cooldowns have shifted during beta, so use current tooltips rather than memorized numbers.

### Tank, Heal, or Deal Damage

- **Retribution:** Straightforward solo and two-handed melee direction.
- **Protection:** Intentional dungeon tanking with threat and shield-oriented tools.
- **Holy:** Group healing with casting and recovery equipment.

All three use Mana, but they do not share one equipment set automatically. Choose the role you will actually perform and prepare for it.

### Professions and Mounts

**Guide recommendations:** Gathering professions can fund equipment and travel; Engineering offers useful gadgets; production professions support crafted supplies. Choose based on what you enjoy and will use.

The class warhorse and later charger milestones can reduce ordinary mount expenses in the supplied Paladin route. Verify their current trainer or quest requirements before relying on a specific cost.

### Dungeon Etiquette

Bring the role you signed up for, let the tank control the approach, watch the healer's Mana, and ask before Need rolls. Redemption and protective Blessings are group recovery tools, not reasons to ignore a bad pull.

## Speed and Efficiency Rules

- [ ] Quest in grouped loops rather than repeated single-objective trips.
- [ ] Prepare dungeon quests before entering; repeat farming is a separate choice.
- [ ] Recover between pulls and use campsites or rested areas when practical.
- [ ] Keep your current weapon and weapon skills suitable for your role.
- [ ] Do not buy every armor upgrade; save for meaningful expenses.
- [ ] Use zeppelins and flight points instead of changing your Hearthstone for every meeting.
- [ ] Use Exorcism and other enemy-specific tools only against valid targets.
- [ ] Pull deliberately; defensive abilities are emergency tools, not a reason to collect extra enemies.

## Route at a Glance

**Deathknell → Brill → Bandarion Keep → Tirisfal completion → Sepulcher and Ruins of Lordaeron → Hillsbrad or the Barrens → wider Horde zones → Riverglades and Krol'dok → Plaguelands and later dungeons → level 60.**
