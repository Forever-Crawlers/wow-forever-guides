# Orc Hunter: Endgame

Normal ruleset — Horde — Orc Hunter. The level-60 continuation of the [1–60 walkthrough](/docs/classes/hunter/orc-1-60). The class-neutral framework lives in [Endgame](/docs/endgame); this page is the hunter-specific layer.

## The Shape of Hunter Endgame

Hunter endgame is four systems, not an endless grind: raids, gear, economy, and reputation. The first raid tier opens December 9, 2026 (Barrow Deeps 10-player, Hyjal Summit 20-player, Onyxia's Lair 40-player), five weeks after launch. A fresh 60 has exactly enough time to run the gear circuit, finish attunements, and find a guild — if none of it is left for raid night.

## Spec and Raid Discipline

Beast Mastery remains the leveling engine, but raiding runs on **Marksmanship** for most rosters and **Survival** for specific debuff slots depending on guild composition. Respec cost is trivial compared to showing up unprepared — confirm the spec with the raid leader.

Raid discipline for Hunters, regardless of spec:

1. **Tranquilizing Shot is a job, not a button.** Any boss that frenzies assigns the dispel to Hunters. Missed tranqs wipe raids.
2. **Threat management is the class identity.** Feign Death on cooldown when high on threat; a Feign Death that prevents a wipe beats any damage meter number.
3. **Stay spread.** Chain lightning, cleaves, and breaths punish clumping. The dead Hunter does zero damage.
4. **Bring a spare weapon.** A broken or disabled ranged weapon (some boss effects target them) means a dead slot — carry a spare bow or staff in the bags.
5. **Ammo is a raid resource.** Stack before raid night; running out mid-boss is a self-inflicted wound.

## Onyxia's Lair (40 players — confirmed first tier)

Classic-derived notes; Forever's version may retune. Verify mechanics on live.

- **Deep Breath:** when Onyxia takes flight, she periodically breathes across the room. Spread out while she is airborne; the breath kills clustered players.
- **Whelps:** eggs hatch in phase two. Assigned AoE handles them; Hunters keep shooting the boss unless assigned.
- **Phase three:** tank picks her up after the landing; threat resets. Feign Death before the land if threat is high.

## Rhok'delar: The Hunter Epic

The classic hunter epic weapon chain — a demon-hunting solo questline that ends with the Rhok'delar bow and Lok'delar staff — is one of the class's defining experiences. **Unverified for Forever's first tier:** the classic chain drops from Molten Core content, which is not announced. If the revamped iconic raid brings it back, this section gets a full walkthrough. Until then, treat it as a rumor to watch.

## The Economy of 60

- Repair bills and ammunition are the baseline; raid consumables stack on top.
- Money in: daily quests, selling runecloth and materials, the auction house.
- Money out: repairs, ammo, consumables, respecs.
- The epic mount (~1,000 gold) is still on the table if it was skipped at 40.

## PvP

Battlegrounds at 60 run Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin, and Alterac Valley. A raiding-geared Hunter is a flag-carrying liability and a tower-defense asset: defend, kite, and Feign Death to break focus fire. Honor grinding is its own ladder — decide early whether the season matters, because the gear path diverges from PvE.

## Guild Trials

The first weeks in a raid guild are a trial: show up on time, know the fights, admit mistakes, and keep the meters honest over the course of a night rather than a single pull. Guilds keep the Hunters who tranq on time and Feign before the wipe, not the ones who top a meter and die to aggro.

When the raid leader says "the guide ends here," that is the compliment.

## Appendix: Legacy Raid Reference (Classic Mechanics)

The following is owner-supplied reference for **Molten Core and Blackwing Lair — raids not announced for Forever's first tier**. It preserves hunter-specific boss notes for when the revamped iconic raid returns. Classic mechanics; expect retuning.

### Molten Core — Hunter Notes (Classic)

| Boss | Hunter job |
| --- | --- |
| Lucifron | Curse and mind-control dispels are group jobs. Steady damage, no heroics. |
| Magmadar | **Tranquilizing Shot duty.** The frenzy dispel is the Hunter's raid-defining job here. |
| Gehennas | Dispel magic/curses on schedule; spread for the shadow bolts. |
| Garr | Banish assignments are warlock business. Kill adds on call, do not multi-shot into banished targets. |
| Baron Geddon | **Move out with Living Bomb.** The player with the bomb runs away — a Hunter who Feigns with it still explodes. |
| Shazzrah | Decurse duty rotates; keep shooting between dispels. |
| Sulfuron / Golemagg | Tank-and-spank with add management. Tranq if a hound frenzies. |
| Majordomo | Crowd control discipline: assigned sheep/banish targets are not for pets or multi-shot. |
| Ragnaros | Melee out on the knockback wave (the " Sons of Flame" add phase in classic). Hunters and casters keep shooting from range the entire fight. |

### Blackwing Lair — Hunter Notes (Classic)

| Boss | Hunter job |
| --- | --- |
| Razorgore | Phase one is orb control and egg breaking. Hunters kill orb controllers and mind-controlled adds on call. |
| Vaelastrasz | The fight is a DPS race with a debuff that makes abilities free and eventually kills the holder. Blow every cooldown; Feign is useless against the debuff timer. |
| Broodlord / Firemaw / Ebonroc / Flamegor | Suppression room discipline: no pets on traps, follow the pull order, tranq the frenzies (Flamegor). |
| Chromaggus | Breath rotations and dispels. Follow the breath call order; steady damage, no heroics. |
| Nefarian | Class calls. **The Hunter call breaks the ranged weapon.** Bring the spare bow or staff; swap when it happens, repair after. Kill the adds on the call. |

Beyond BWL, Zul'Gurub and Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj are 20-player bridge tiers; Temple of Ahn'Qiraj and Naxxramas sit beyond. The same discipline scales: tranq on time, Feign early, stay spread.
