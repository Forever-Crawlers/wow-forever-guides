# Fury Warrior (Berserker): Build Guide

Dual wield. Fast swings. A Rage bar that fills with every critical strike. Fury is the berserker option of the Warrior class — the fastest, most reactive rotation in the class, the best cleave, and a kit that gives nothing back for survival.

This is the build guide: talent path, rotation, rage economy, stats, and races. For the level-by-level road, read [Troll Warrior: Levels 1–60](/docs/classes/warrior/troll-1-60) — the two pair naturally, since Fury comes online around 20 and owns the dungeon DPS slot from 30 onward.

Rule of thumb: the rage rework, Victory Rush, Whirlwind behavior, and the new talent names are solid. Numbers can move.

## The Identity

Fury plays where the rage is. Every auto-attack — main hand and off-hand — builds the bar; crits build it double; Lingering Rage keeps it from draining after combat. Where Arms pools and bursts, Fury spends constantly. The spec feels like a fire that has to be fed, and the feeding is the fun.

The trade is honest: no self-heal outside Victory Rush and Gore Drinker, no shield, no escape. Fury Warriors die when they overpull or when the healer looks away. The spec rewards aggression with limits — pull two, cleave them down; pull four, run back.

## The Build at 30

The beta cap sits at 30, and 30 is also where the spec unlocks: 21 talent points in, Berserker Stance in hand, Death Wish on the bar. Reference allocation from community calculators (Babyhoof/Wowhead build via the WoW Forever talent calculator):

| Order | Talent | Ranks | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cruelty | 5/5 | Crit is damage and rage economy in one stat. First five points, no debate. |
| 2 | Lingering Rage | 2/5 | Rage survives between pulls. Two points is the sweet spot before better nodes open. |
| 3 | Unbridled Wrath | 5/5 | Extra rage on swings; dual wield means twice the proc surface. |
| 4 | Furious Precision | 3/3 | Off-hand hit chance, up to 10%. Directly attacks the dual wield tax. |
| 5 | Dual Wield Specialization | 5/5 | Off-hand damage. The reason to be Fury. |
| 6 | Death Wish | 1/1 | +20% physical damage for the cost of taking 5% more. The burst button and the leveling panic button. |

Two routes, both 0/21/0 at 30:

- **Solo route:** two-handed weapon plus Death Wish. Simpler rage curve, Victory Rush does the healing.
- **Dungeon route:** dual wield plus Furious Precision. The role the spec is built for — sustained melee damage on the boss while Cleave and Whirlwind cover the trash.

From 30 the tree fills toward the real capstones: Flurry for the haste engine, Gore Drinker for sustain, Bloodthirst as the spender identity, Improved Whirlwind for the cleave crown. At 60 the shape is deep Fury with an Arms or Protection dip for utility — exact split per sim results at cap.

## The Rage Economy

Everything Fury does starts here, and Forever rebuilt the rules.

- **Rage is normalized.** Weapon speed does not change rage generation. Weapon *choice* is a damage decision, not a resource one.
- **Crits generate double rage.** Crit is both a damage stat and a resource stat. Cruelty first is not tradition — it is arithmetic.
- **Lingering Rage delays decay.** Ten seconds at full rank means the bar arrives at the next pull already warm. Never fight the first ten seconds on an empty bar.
- **Reckless Swings feeds the fire.** Next four attacks generate 5 bonus rage each, for 25% of base health. Press it before Execute windows and hard pulls, not after the bar is full.
- **Berserker Rage** breaks Fear and Incapacitate and adds rage from incoming hits. In Berserker Stance it is both a survival tool and a resource tool. Casters feared you in Classic; in Forever you walk out of it angrier.
- **Spend above the stance floor.** Stance swaps cost rage. Pool above 10 before dancing to Battle Stance for Overpower, and come back with enough to keep spending.

## Rotation

### Single Target

1. **Battle Shout** up, always. The cheapest damage in the kit.
2. **Rend** — swap to Battle Stance for it. In Forever it also feeds Arms-side talents some Fury builds splash; even pure Fury keeps it for rage efficiency on long fights.
3. **Bloodrage** on the opener to guarantee the first spend.
4. **Sunder Armor** to five stacks when grouped; skip solo.
5. **Execute** under 20% health — 15 rage, finishing only. Dumping 40 rage into Execute is rage the next mob will miss.
6. **Heroic Strike** only to dump excess rage above 60.
7. **Bloodthirst** on cooldown once the tree reaches it.
8. **Whirlwind** on cooldown. Both weapons hit; it is never just an AoE button.
9. **Overpower** on every dodge — swap to Battle Stance, spend, swap back.
10. **Death Wish** for tough fights and burn windows. The 5% damage taken is real; press it with a plan.

### Multiple Targets

- **Whirlwind first.** Both weapons, one global. The post-rework default.
- **Cleave as the rage dump** on two targets; its cost is discounted by Raging Blows.
- **Thunder Clap on three or more** beats Cleave for efficiency and softens the pack.
- **Sweeping Strikes does not live here.** That is the Arms toy. Fury's cleave is native — Whirlwind and Cleave are the whole plan, and the plan is strong.

### Situational

- **Reckless Swings** when the bar runs dry mid-fight. Health is a resource too; spend it like one.
- **Gore Drinker procs are the heal.** Enrage effects and Bloodthirst empower the next three swings to return health. Spend them on something that lives long enough to pay out.
- **Shield Bash needs a shield** until Pummel at 38. Keep a one-hander and board in the bags for caster interrupts; after 38, geometry stops being the interrupt.
- **Intercept** is the gap closer and peel-breaker from 30. Berserker Stance only — plan the stance before planning the chase.

## Stats and Gear

1. **Hit.** Dual wield pays a miss tax on both hands; Furious Precision refunds part of the off-hand share. Hit to the effective cap before anything else.
2. **Critical strike.** Double rage on crits turns crit into the Fury engine stat. Flurry at cap makes crit self-fueling: crit faster, swing faster, crit more.
3. **Attack Power / Strength.** Honest damage. Two AP per Strength.
4. **Stamina and Spirit by context.** Dungeons want Stamina; solo wants Spirit for the between-pull regen that stacks with Victory Rush.

Gear rules:

- **Two one-handers of matched speed** — Whirlwind swings both, and rage is normalized, so slow-fast pairings no longer matter for rage. Weapon damage per swing still matters for instant attacks.
- **Weapon upgrades on schedule** are the second talent tree. Auction or dungeon drops near 20, 30, 40, and the 50s.
- **Sharpening stones always.** Cheap, significant, always on. Warriors are a supply chain with a health bar.

## Races

- **Orc (Horde):** Blood Fury adds 10% attack power for 15 seconds — a burst window on demand that lines up with Death Wish. Hardiness shortens stuns. The default Fury pick.
- **Human (Alliance):** sword and mace skill bonuses refund hit that gear would otherwise have to carry. Best PvE racial for a dual wielder; the PvP pick on any race with Escape Artist access.
- **Troll (Horde):** Berserking is 15% attack speed — attack speed is rage generation, so it is a resource cooldown pressed every fight. Pairs with the Regeneration/Spirit sustain package. Strong leveling choice, competitive at cap.

Endgame damage between these is close enough that play outweighs racial. Pick for the fantasy or the utility, not the sim.

## The Road to 30

Fury is not the level-1 spec. It is the level-20-and-onward spec.

| Range | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 1–20 | Arms highway with a two-hander: Improved Rend, Deflection, Improved Overpower, Improved Charge, Deep Wounds. Weapon damage per swing is the leveling damage. |
| 20 | Train Dual Wield. Equip two one-handers. Victory Rush arrives the same level — the kill-chain sustain engine turns on, and Fury becomes viable from this point. |
| 24 | Execute. Finisher only, cheap. |
| 30 | Berserker Stance quest on Fray Island (near Ratchet), then Berserker Rage and Intercept from the trainer. Respec into the Fury build above or keep Arms talents and let gear carry the dual wield — both work until 36. |
| 36 | Whirlwind. Both weapons. The spec's identity is fully online. |
| 38 | Pummel. The real interrupt; the shield stays in the bags. |

Launch-week wisdom from the leveling guides: Arms and Protection both level well, and a respec around 40 is common. Fury's case is the dungeon DPS case — if the run is dungeon-heavy from 30, Fury earns its keep earlier than the solo-focused routes suggest.

## Weaknesses, Honestly

- **No defensive kit.** Victory Rush and Gore Drinker are it. Overpulls are deaths, not setbacks.
- **Gear-hungry.** Two weapons to upgrade instead of one; hit to buy that Arms does not fully need.
- **Rage can still starve.** Normalized rage fixes weapon speed, not bad play. Empty-bar openers remain the worst moments of the spec.
- **Beta data.** The tree was rebuilt once already this cycle; it can be rebuilt again.
