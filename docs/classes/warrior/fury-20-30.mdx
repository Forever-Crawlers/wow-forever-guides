# Fury Warrior: Levels 20–30

Normal ruleset — Horde or Alliance — Fury Warrior. The setup band: Dual Wield and Victory Rush arrive together at 20, the rage economy matures through the mid-20s, and 30 opens Berserker Stance, Intercept, Berserker Rage, and Death Wish all at once. This guide runs the band on the Fury talent path, not the Arms highway.

Two honest warnings before starting. First, Fury at 20–30 is a promise, not a payoff — the spec's identity buttons (Whirlwind at 36, Bloodthirst later) have not arrived yet, and Arms with a two-hander is the smoother solo road in this band. What Fury banks here is the engine: dual wield rage, crit-fueled spending, and a finished 21-point core at 30. Second, Fury costs two weapon upgrades instead of one. If the auction house is poor on one-handers, stay Arms until 30 and respec — the build loses nothing by waiting.

Pairs naturally with [Troll Warrior: Levels 1–60](/docs/classes/warrior/troll-1-60) for zone specifics, and hands off to [Fury Warrior (Berserker): Build Guide](/docs/classes/warrior/builds/fury) for the level-30 build in full.

## Act 1 — Level 20: The Dual Wield Switch, Levels 20–21

Twenty is the fork. The trainer teaches Dual Wield, Cleave, Victory Rush, and Slam in one visit, and the character changes species: from a slow two-hand poker into a blender that never stops swinging.

**Goal:** level 21, two one-handers equipped, Victory Rush reflex installed, talent respec done if coming from Arms.

- [ ] Visit the trainer. Dual Wield first, then Cleave, Victory Rush, Slam. Rank-ups on everything else while there.
- [ ] Equip the two best one-handers owned. Weapon speed does not matter for rage anymore — normalized — so pick raw damage per swing. Matched speeds are a small bonus for instant-attack feel, not a rule.
- [ ] Respec if leveling Arms to this point. The Fury path starts now and the points below assume it.
- [ ] Relearn the pull. Fury at 20 has less burst than a 2H Arms opener: Charge in, Battle Shout, Rend, and let the swings build. First ten seconds feel weaker; the last ten feel endless.
- [ ] Install the Victory Rush reflex: after every killing blow, under 90% health, press it. It is a 10% heal wearing a damage costume. The spec's uptime comes from chaining kills, not from bandages.
- [ ] Keep one bandage habit: bandage between pulls when Victory Rush is unavailable (no corpse nearby). Fury sustains through volume — never stop moving.

**Talent plan, 20–21:** by 21 the tree holds Cruelty 5/5 and Unbridled Wrath 4/5 (points from 10 onward: Cruelty 10–14, Lingering Rage 15–16, Unbridled Wrath 17–21). Crit first — crit is double rage, and double rage is the whole economy.

**Gear check:** two one-handers, highest damage per swing affordable. Flurry Axe, Thrash Blade quest chain, or auction blues near 20 — any of them carries. Shield stays in the bags for now: Shield Bash is still the only interrupt until 38.

**What this act teaches:** Fury's contract — weaker openers, stronger finishes. Where Arms front-loads a fight into a Charge and a Rend, Fury wins the fight that starts at 40% health and ends with the rage bar full. The blender starts slow and never stops.

## Act 2 — Levels 22–24: Execute and the Wailing Caverns Close-Out

The early 20s give the finisher and the last dungeon of the band. Wailing Caverns sits at 17–24: do it once here, quest-loaded, and let Victory Rush chain the deviate packs.

**Goal:** level 24, Execute learned, WC cleared once, Furious Precision started.

- [ ] Learn Piercing Howl at 22 if trained-packs keep escaping; otherwise skip it — the Fury bar has better uses.
- [ ] Run Wailing Caverns once, every quest accepted, one clean clear. DPS slot or tank slot both work; Fury holds threat poorly, so let the tank open and give the rage two seconds to settle.
- [ ] Cleave is the two-target button now. Pull pairs deliberately: Fury kills two mobs faster than most classes kill one, and Victory Rush pays out twice.
- [ ] Learn Execute at 24. Finisher only — 15 rage, nothing more. Dumping the bar into Execute is rage the next pull will miss.
- [ ] Train Sunder Armor rank-ups if dungeoning again soon; skip otherwise. Fury's job in a dungeon is damage, not stacks.
- [ ] Southern Barrens and Stonetalon carry these levels. Camp Taurajo is the southern hub — set the flight path before questing.

**Talent plan, 22–24:** finish Unbridled Wrath 5/5 at 21, then Furious Precision 1/3 at 22, 2/3 at 23, 3/3 at 24. Off-hand hit is the dual wield tax; every point here is refunded damage.

**Gear check:** weapon upgrade audit. The 20–24 band hands out quests with one-hander rewards on both coasts; the auction house fills gaps. If neither one-hander is within a few levels, buy — dual wield punishes stale weapons twice.

**What this act teaches:** Fury's first identity is volume. Two mobs, three mobs, back-to-back pulls with no pause — the spec converts aggression into health through Victory Rush and into rage through Unbridled Wrath. Boredom is the only real danger in this band.

## Act 3 — Levels 25–27: Hillsbrad and the Mid-Band Grind

The mid-20s widen the map: Hillsbrad (Tarren Mill) is the classic Horde road, Stonetalon the western alternative. Pick one per session; both feed the same talent finish lines.

**Goal:** level 27, Dual Wield Specialization finished, hit the quest-dense circuits.

- [ ] Take Tarren Mill's quest board and run the Hillsbrad circuits: farms, murlocs, the yeti cave. Humans and murlocs die to volume — exactly Fury's game.
- [ ] Campfire every session. Cooking's Basic Campfire Kit plus a profession fixture; sit, buff, go. The crit fixtures pair with Cruelty: crit is rage, rage is everything.
- [ ] Well Fed always. The 5% kill XP from cooked food is the strongest leveling consumable in Forever and it costs silver.
- [ ] Watch for casters. Pre-38 Warriors have no interrupt without a shield swap. Line-of-sight pulls around buildings and fences; let the caster walk into the blender instead of casting at it from range.
- [ ] Hamstring everything that runs. A fleeing mob pulling a second camp is how Fury dies: no Shield Wall, no escape button, just speed and rage.

**Talent plan, 25–29:** Dual Wield Specialization 1/5 at 25 through 5/5 at 29. The off-hand goes from decoration to weapon.

**Gear check:** sharpening stones on both weapons, always. Two stones, two weapons, still cheap. Spirit pieces quietly extend the Victory Rush chain — health regen between pulls means arriving at the next mob full.

**What this act teaches:** the mid-band is where the economy compounds. Unbridled Wrath feeds crits into rage, Dual Wield Spec turns the off-hand into a second paycheck, and Lingering Rage means the bar never fully empties between pulls. By 27 the spec stops feeling like two weapons and starts feeling like one engine.

## Act 4 — Levels 28–29: The Dalaran Check, Levels 28–29

Twenty-eight opens a choice: the new Forever content in the City of Dalaran (28–33), or the classic roads. Dalaran is good experience and hostile territory for the Horde — worth exactly one quest-loaded visit, grouped.

**Goal:** level 29, Dual Wield Specialization 5/5, group assembled for Dalaran or the decision made to skip it.

- [ ] Decide on Dalaran early. The guards hate the Horde; go grouped, go summoned if possible, one clear, leave with the quest turn-ins.
- [ ] If skipping Dalaran, Arathi Highlands and Desolace previews fill 28–29 alongside Hillsbrad's last circuits. Hammerfall flight path if passing.
- [ ] Pool rage before named mobs and quest elites: kill the guard mob next to the elite first, walk in with a full bar, and the fight ends before Death Wish would even matter.
- [ ] Stock the 30 fund. Fray Island is a combat quest — potions, bandages, food, a sharpening stone charge or two. Arriving at 30 prepared is half the fight.
- [ ] Pre-position: be in the Barrens or with a hearth set near Ratchet before dinging 30. The stance quest runs from Ratchet's dock.

**Talent plan, 28–29:** finish Dual Wield Specialization (5/5 at 29). The 21-point core at 30 is one point away.

**Gear check:** best available one-handers, both slots. The 30 Whirlwind weapon quest is a two-hander — Fury does not need it, and the reagent hunt is a detour. Auction one-handers at 30–32 are the Fury play; skip the axe without regret.

**What this act teaches:** the end of a band is a staging problem, not a grinding problem. Positioning, potions, and a hearth near Fray Island matter more than the last half-level.

## Act 5 — Level 30: Fray Island and the Payoff

Thirty is the biggest single-level spike a Warrior gets. Berserker Stance from the Fray Island quest, Berserker Rage and Intercept from the trainer, and the 21st talent point buys Death Wish. Fury goes from a promise to a spec in one session.

**Goal:** level 30, Berserker Stance earned, Death Wish taken, the new bar rehearsed.

- [ ] Take the Berserker Stance quest from the class trainer. It runs to Fray Island in the Barrens, south of Ratchet — a short boat or swim, then a gauntlet fight.
- [ ] Go prepared: potions on the bar, bandages hot, rage pooled before every wave of the gauntlet. The fight is honest at 30 but it is not free.
- [ ] Learn Berserker Rage and Intercept immediately after. Berserker Rage breaks Fear and Incapacitate and pumps rage from incoming hits — press it when afraid and when empty. Intercept is the second gap closer, Berserker Stance only.
- [ ] Spend the 30 talent point: **Death Wish** (Fury tier 7, 20 points required — the build above arrives with exactly 20). +20% physical damage for the cost of taking 5% more. The burst button, the elite button, the panic-in-reverse button.
- [ ] Rehearse the new opener: Charge (Battle Stance) → Battle Shout → Rend → swap to Berserker Stance above 10 rage → spend, spend, spend. Tactical Mastery at 14 keeps the swap affordable.
- [ ] Hamstring plus Intercept is the new escape: root the chaser, intercept a neutral mob at range, keep running.
- [ ] Do not buy the Arms fantasy. Sweeping Strikes, Spearing Strike, the Whirlwind Axe — that is the other road. Fury's toys are Flurry, Bloodthirst, and a second weapon; they arrive on schedule and this guide's job ends with the engine built.

**Talent plan at 30:** the 21-point core, complete — Cruelty 5/5, Lingering Rage 2/5, Unbridled Wrath 5/5, Furious Precision 3/3, Dual Wield Specialization 5/5, Death Wish 1/1. From 31 the tree walks toward Flurry, Gore Drinker, and Bloodthirst; the build guide holds the full map.

**Gear check:** two fresh one-handers (auction or dungeon), both stoned. Death Wish on the bar next to Berserker Rage — the two buttons are a pair: one pays health for damage, the other refunds rage for getting hit.

**What this act teaches:** 30 is where Fury's ledger balances. The band invested crit, hit, and off-hand damage into an engine that hummed without its identity buttons; the stance quest, Intercept, Berserker Rage, and Death Wish arrive as the interest payment. From here to 36 the only job is feeding the fire.

## Fury Leveling Systems, 20–30

### The Talent Path in Full

| Level | Point | Running total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10–14 | Cruelty 1–5 | 5 |
| 15–16 | Lingering Rage 1–2 | 7 |
| 17–21 | Unbridled Wrath 1–5 | 12 |
| 22–24 | Furious Precision 1–3 | 15 |
| 25–29 | Dual Wield Specialization 1–5 | 20 |
| 30 | Death Wish | 21 |

Crit first, hit second, off-hand damage third, burst last. The order is the economy: every node pays for the next one.

### Victory Rush Discipline

Kill-chain healing, not a rotation filler. After a killing blow, under 90% health, press it — the heal is the point, the damage is a bonus. Between pulls, bandage when no corpse is available. Eating is for dungeon breaks.

### Cleave and the Two-Target Rule

From 20, pulling two mobs is standard operating procedure, not a risk. Cleave spends the rage, Victory Rush refunds the health, and Unbridled Wrath keeps the bar alive. The rule inverts the Arms instinct: Arms avoids the second mob until Sweeping Strikes; Fury invites it.

### Stances at 30

- **Battle Stance:** home until 30. Overpower and Rend live here.
- **Defensive Stance:** never quest here; dungeon emergency only.
- **Berserker Stance:** from 30, the spend stance. Intercept, Berserker Rage, Whirlwind later. Swap above 10 rage, always.

### Money and Mounts

Fury spends like the rest of the class: weapons first, everything else second. Two weapon slots means the auction tax doubles — bank silver from Hillsbrad onward and treat the 40 raptor as the only mandatory large purchase. Sell cloth, sell ore, buy nothing that does not swing.

## Speed Rules for the Band

- [ ] **Two one-handers, always current.** Dual wield punishes stale weapons twice. Auction at 20, audit at 25, refresh at 30.
- [ ] **Victory Rush after every kill.** Under 90% health, on cooldown. The heal is the rotation.
- [ ] **Pull pairs, not singles.** Fury's two-target math beats every solo spec in the band.
- [ ] **Execute cheap.** Fifteen rage, finishing blow only.
- [ ] **Campfire and Well Fed every session.** Crit fixtures pair with Cruelty; 5% XP is 5% XP.
- [ ] **Line-of-sight casters.** No Pummel until 38; geometry is the interrupt.
- [ ] **Pool rage before elites.** Empty-bar Fury loses fights it should win.
- [ ] **Stage level 29 for level 30.** Hearth near Ratchet, potions stocked, Fray Island mapped.

## The Route at a Glance

Dual Wield and Victory Rush at 20 → WC once, quest-loaded → two one-handers on schedule → Hillsbrad volume grinding through the mid-20s → Dalaran check at 28, once, grouped → staged 29 → Fray Island, Berserker Stance, Death Wish at 30 — and the engine that carries Fury to Whirlwind at 36 is built.
