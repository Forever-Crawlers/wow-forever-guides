# Professions

Professions are character skills that gather and craft. They fund the level-40 mount, fill gear gaps, and feed the strongest leveling consumables. This page covers the system; the [FAQ](/docs/reference/faq) answers the common specific questions.

## The Slots

- **Two primary professions** per character: gathering, crafting, or one of each.
- **Three secondary professions** — Fishing, Cooking, First Aid — every character takes all of them. They do not count against the primary slots.

Swapping a primary is free and instant (spellbook → Unlearn), but all skill and recipes are lost permanently. Choose like it is permanent.

## Pairings

| Combination | Logic |
| --- | --- |
| Skinning + Leatherworking | Turn every beast killed into material, then into armor and bags. The classic self-sufficiency pair for leather wearers. |
| Mining + Blacksmithing | Ore into weapons, armor, and sharpening stones. The warrior pair; also feeds Engineering. |
| Herbalism + Alchemy | Herbs into potions and flasks. Consumable supply for life, strong at 60. |
| Tailoring + Enchanting | Cloth into bags and gear; disenchanting greens into materials. Low material cost, high utility. |
| Double gathering (Skinning + Mining, or Herbalism + Mining) | Maximum income while leveling. Everything sells. |

## Gathering Professions While Leveling

Gathering is the mount fund. Skinning turns every beast corpse into loot; Mining and Herbalism add nodes along every road. Trainer locations are in the [FAQ](/docs/reference/faq). Rules:

- Train the gathering profession immediately at the start zone; train the crafting profession at 10–12.
- Gathering tools (Skinning Knife, Mining Pick, Blacksmith Hammer) are **passive in bags** — buy them once, never equip them. Fishing Poles must be equipped.
- Skinning requires looting the corpse first, then clicking the skinning icon.
- Visit profession trainers every five skill points; falling behind locks recipes.

## Campfire Fixtures

Forever-specific: at **profession skill 20**, each primary unlocks a campfire fixture. Placing a fixture at a campfire grants a buff:

| Profession | Fixture | Buff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leatherworking | Camp Tent | Rested-XP storage — log out at a tent to bank rest anywhere, and gain bonus XP while it lasts |
| Blacksmithing | Sharpening Wheel | Weapon damage bonus for the party |
| Cooking | Basic Campfire Kit | Enables campfires; Well Fed food grants +5% kill XP |

Key rule: fixture buffs scale with **character level**, not profession skill. A level-20 profession skill on a level-30 character still grants a level-30 buff. Level both primaries to skill 20 as early as possible — it is the cheapest power in the game.

## Secondaries: Not Optional

| Profession | Why it is mandatory |
| --- | --- |
| **First Aid** | Bandages are the heal between pulls for every class without healing. Warriors live on bandages until the 40s. Level it without mercy. |
| **Cooking** | Well Fed food is +5% kill XP — the strongest leveling consumable. Also required for campfires. |
| **Fishing** | Feeds Cooking. Take it when a trainer is convenient, not before. |

## The Money Method

1. Sell everything grey.
2. Auction every green item the character cannot use; vendor the rest.
3. Keep leather, hides, and 2–3 stacks of each cloth type; sell the surplus.
4. Two money landmarks: the level-40 mount (~100 gold total) and the level-60 epic mount (~1,000 gold).

Crafting gold sinks come second to the mount fund until 40.
