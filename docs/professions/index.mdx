## Roadmap

- Choosing two primary professions for your character and goals
- Gathering and crafting routes with current Forever recipes
- Cooking, Fishing, and First Aid for new players
- Campsite objects and shared profession benefits
- Class-friendly pairings, including Skinning and Leatherworking for Hunters

The [Horde New Player Guide](/docs/new-player/horde) already covers the basic profession interface and where to begin.
