# Horde new-player content design

## Intent and why

Turn the stock Docusaurus site into a useful guide library for a first-time Horde player, then provide a complete Orc Hunter path for levels 1–20. Use the supplied `WoW_Forever_Complete_Guide.docx` for its beginner-friendly voice and progression, but do not copy older Classic mechanics when current Forever sources disagree.

The reader should always know what to read now, what can wait, and which future topics are not written yet.

The supplied source file is `WoW_Forever_Complete_Guide.docx`. Its text has been extracted without changing the original to `.superpowers/research/wow-forever-complete-guide.txt` for the implementation session.

## Goals

- Replace the stock tutorial documentation with a progressive guide structure.
- Publish one complete, source-reviewed new-player guide applicable to every Horde race and class.
- Publish one complete Orc Hunter guide for levels 1–20, including the first pet, early dungeon etiquette, and level-20 handoff.
- Add visible, useful placeholders for common future sections instead of empty pages or misleading links.
- Keep internal planning documents out of the public documentation sidebar and routes.
- Preserve the Torchlit Crossover theme and automatic GitHub Pages deployment.
- Verify internal links, TypeScript, production output, and the deployed pages.

## Non-goals and anti-goals

- No full 21–60 route, endgame guide, or additional class/race guide in this pass.
- No copied route text or proprietary images from other guide sites.
- No new dependencies, interactive tools, or test files.
- No empty placeholder headings presented as completed instructions.
- No outdated Classic advice presented as current Forever fact.
- No undocumented claims about contested Orc racials or uncertain future content.

## Research findings

- [Blizzard's official starter guide](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24315280/new-players-starter-guide-welcome-to-world-of-warcraft-forever) orders onboarding by access, experience preset, ruleset, character creation, quests/UI, combat/death, gear/bags, professions, social play, and exploration. It confirms Enhanced as a friendly default, same-ruleset/faction grouping, two-name characters, campsites, and current Forever interface basics.
- [Wowhead's Hunter leveling guide](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/classes/hunter/leveling-tips) uses an early “do not miss” summary and first-ten-levels explanation before talents, pets, rotation, trainer visits, weapons/gear, professions, and zones. This is a useful class-guide pattern, but its mechanics are Classic and require current verification.
- [Icy Veins' Forever Hunter guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/beast-mastery-hunter-leveling) separates talents, rotation, abilities, stats, zones, tips, milestones, and professions. It documents current Forever details including Mana, pet families, campsites, and level-based unlocks.
- [Maxroll's Forever Hunter guide](https://maxroll.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/hunter-leveling-guide) emphasizes unlocks, the level-10 Taming the Beast questline, pet training/feeding, and trainer-visit milestones.
- [Warcraft Tavern's Horde route](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/horde-leveling-guide/) demonstrates level-banded navigation, guidelines, purchase lists, and explicit race entry points. Its exact Classic route is not a Forever source.
- [WoW Forever Guides' library](https://wowforeverguides.com/guides) separates Start Here, Races, Leveling, World/Endgame, Professions/Gear, Systems, and Tools. Its checked dates and evidence labels are useful trust signals.
- [RaidGuild's handbook](https://handbook.raidguild.org/docs/overview/intro) separates orientation, communication, membership, activity procedures, and resources. [Ministry of Defense's guild rules](https://www.ministryofdefense.net/raiding/raiding-rules-and-regulations/) separate conduct, attendance, communication, roles, and preparation. For this site, that supports putting social etiquette and “where to ask for help” in shared onboarding rather than repeating it in every class guide.

## Approaches

- **Selected: progressive guide paths.** Shared Horde onboarding leads to a race/class-specific path, followed by later leveling, dungeon, profession, endgame, and reference sections. It matches the approved scope and avoids duplicating shared material.
- **Rejected: mirror the DOCX's four parts.** The draft's Start Here / Road to 60 / Endgame / Reference progression is useful, but a direct copy would mix shared and Orc-specific material and make additional race/class guides repetitive.
- **Rejected: flat topic wiki.** Flexible for reference, but does not answer a beginner's “what do I read next?”

## Information architecture

Use autogenerated Docusaurus navigation with explicit positions and labels. Remove `docs/tutorial-basics/` and `docs/tutorial-extras/` from the website. Add `exclude: ['superpowers/**']` to the classic docs preset so committed planning records remain in Git but are not published as guides.

Top-level order:

1. **Start Here** — `docs/intro.mdx`, a short library welcome linking to the Horde guide and Orc Hunter guide.
2. **New Player** — `docs/new-player/horde.mdx`, the complete shared guide.
3. **Races** — a planned hub explaining future race-specific onboarding.
4. **Classes** — a class hub plus the Hunter category and completed `Orc Hunter: Levels 1–20` guide.
5. **Leveling** — a planned hub for faction routes, level bands, travel, rested XP, and future 21–60 content.
6. **Dungeons** — a planned hub for first-run preparation, Ragefire Chasm, Wailing Caverns, roles, and loot etiquette.
7. **Professions** — a planned hub for profession choice, leveling, campsites, and secondary professions.
8. **Endgame** — a planned hub for level-60 gearing, raids, PvP, and guild preparation.
9. **Reference** — a planned hub for glossary, UI/keybinds, items/loot, macros/addons, and account safety.

Each category has a `_category_.json` with its label/position. Placeholder hubs contain a short “Planned” admonition, a paragraph about the section's intended job, and a bulleted topic roadmap. They do not pretend to be finished guides and do not link to nonexistent pages. The Classes hub links only to the real Hunter guide; other class names remain plain-text future topics.

Update the navbar's stock “Tutorial” label to “Guides.” Leave the existing homepage hero destination and blog behavior unchanged.

## Shared Horde new-player guide

Audience: someone starting any Horde race/class on Forever, especially a first-time MMO player. Use plain language, short sections, and checklists. State that examples use Normal ruleset and that players should choose with friends before character creation.

Content order:

1. **Before you create a character:** subscription/Game Time, Battle.net installation, select Forever, agree on Horde and the same ruleset with friends, Normal as the learning-friendly choice.
2. **Choose the presentation and character:** Enhanced as the recommended starting preset with settings changeable later, HD/SD as cosmetic choices, race vs. class responsibilities, and the unique two-name combination.
3. **Learn the screen and first quest:** health/resource/action bars, quest tracker/log, map/minimap, spellbook, character panel, and current official hotkeys L, M, P, C, and K.
4. **The basic play loop:** accept quests, read/track objectives, pull one enemy, loot, return for rewards, equip suitable upgrades, and visit class trainers.
5. **Stay alive and recover:** enemies can attack while away, resource bars differ by class, food/drink and safe logout locations, corpse return, and Spirit Healer tradeoffs.
6. **Bags, vendors, and money:** sell gray items, use Buyback for mistakes, repair at anvil vendors, save for training/bags/travel, and avoid unsupported exact prices.
7. **Travel and rested time:** flight masters, Hearthstone binding, roads vs. dangerous shortcuts, and inns/cities for rested XP.
8. **Professions and campsites:** two primary and three secondary professions, current K shortcut, gathering/crafting roles, shared campsite benefits, and contributing profession-made objects.
9. **Playing with others:** party roles, patient communication, asking before Need rolls, guilds as social/help hubs, and where to ask questions. Do not invent Forever-specific chat commands or guild rules.
10. **First-session checklist:** from entering the world through first quest, combat, vendor, Hearthstone, safe logout, and choosing the relevant class guide.
11. **Next step:** link the completed Orc Hunter guide and label other race/class paths as planned.
12. **Sources and review date:** cite Blizzard's starter guide and record `2026-11-04`.

## Orc Hunter levels 1–20 guide

Audience: a new player who chose Orc Hunter. This is a complete early path, not an optimized speedrun. Preserve the DOCX's reassuring voice and habit-building, but correct Classic-only mechanics. Hunters use Mana in Forever; early Hunters wear leather; current milestone references take priority over the DOCX.

Content order:

1. **What to expect:** ranged combat, no permanent pet before 10, Beast Mastery as a forgiving leveling direction, ammo/pet supplies, and a concise level-20 goal.
2. **Core habits:** pull one enemy, let Auto Shot work, manage Mana rather than spending every ability on cooldown, loot/sell/repair/restock, and keep a current ranged weapon.
3. **Levels 1–4 — Valley of Trials:** first quests, ranged opening followed by melee/Raptor Strike when enemies close, level-4 Serpent Sting, bag/vendor habits, and safe single pulls.
4. **Levels 5–7 — early Durotar and Sen'jin:** hub loop, Hearthstone/vendors/flight points where available, Arcane Shot/Hunter's Mark at 6, and leather/Agility/ranged-weapon upgrade basics.
5. **Levels 8–10 — Razor Hill:** Concussive Shot at 8, talent introduction, ammunition/training preparation, and reaching 10.
6. **Level 10 — first pet:** the Orc/Troll Taming the Beast quest at Razor Hill, completing its questline and capital-city follow-up for Feed Pet/Beast Training, choosing a convenient nearby beast rather than chasing a rare “perfect” pet, feeding/happiness, pet threat, and learning pet abilities through taming.
7. **Levels 10–12 — Crossroads:** travel, flight point/Hearthstone, quest batching, ammo/pet food, and the cadence of sending the pet first and allowing it to establish threat.
8. **Levels 13–16 — early Barrens and optional Ragefire Chasm:** directional quest loops, rested XP, the level-14 Cozy Sleeping Bag lead, first-dungeon preparation, tank-led pulls, disabling pet taunt/Growl, and asking about loot.
9. **Levels 17–20 — Wailing Caverns and the level-20 handoff:** optional dungeon quest run, Multi-Shot at 18, and current level-20 unlocks including Aspect of the Cheetah, Aimed Shot, and Freezing Trap.
10. **Gear, talents, and professions summary:** weapon damage and Agility, Beast Mastery as the beginner-friendly tree without an exhaustive point-by-point build, Skinning/Leatherworking as a natural pair, and secondary professions/campsite participation.
11. **Level-20 checklist and future path:** current weapon, ammo, fed pet, trained abilities, flight points, dungeon etiquette, and a clearly labeled roadmap for future 21–30, 31–40, 41–50, 51–60, and Hunter endgame guides.
12. **Sources and review date:** cite Blizzard, Icy Veins, and Maxroll; record `2026-11-04`. Avoid the contested Orc Command racial claim and unsupported Classic prices/routes.

## Placeholder and sourcing policy

- Use “Planned” or “Roadmap,” never “Coming soon” without describing what belongs there.
- Every placeholder explains intended coverage and points back to a real available guide.
- Prefer official Blizzard sources for shared systems. Use current Forever community guides for class milestones and identify them as community sources.
- Do not copy source wording or quest-by-quest routes. Synthesize instructions for this site's beginner audience.
- When reputable sources conflict, omit the contested detail or state the uncertainty; do not guess.
- Keep a short Sources section on each completed guide so later updates have a verification trail.
