# Glossary and Quick Reference

Lookup, not reading. Open this page when a word or system confuses you. Class-specific details live in the class guides; this page is the shared foundation.

## Combat Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Aggro** | A mob deciding to attack. "Having aggro" means it is hitting you. |
| **Threat** | The hidden score that decides who a mob attacks. Tanks build it on purpose; damage dealers manage theirs. |
| **Pull** | Starting a fight, ideally with one mob, on your terms. |
| **Add** | An extra mob that joins a fight uninvited. |
| **Patrol** | Mobs walking a fixed route. Watch the path; pull in the gap. |
| **Leash** | Mobs give up chasing past a distance and walk home with reset health. |
| **Kite** | Killing a mob while running away from it. Traps and slows make this work. |
| **DoT** | Damage over time, ticking after the initial hit. |
| **HoT** | Heal over time. |
| **AoE** | Area-of-effect damage hitting multiple targets. Dangerous near neutral factions. |
| **CC** | Crowd control: freezing, stunning, or disabling a mob out of the fight. |
| **Wipe** | The whole group dies. Happens. Run back together. |
| **Respawn** | Dead mobs return after a few minutes. Never fight on top of a respawn point. |
| **Corpse run** | Walking back to the body as a ghost. Free. Always prefer this. |
| **Rez sickness** | Penalty after spirit-healer resurrection: weakness plus extra gear damage. A fine for impatience. |
| **FD** | Feign Death (Hunter): fake death, dropping all threat. The best panic button in the game. |

## Items and Economy

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Quality tiers** | Grey = vendor trash. White = basic. Green = uncommon (first real gear). Blue = rare (dungeon gear). Purple = epic (raid gear). |
| **BoP / BoE** | Binds on Pickup (untradeable once looted) / Bind on Equip (tradeable until first equipped). |
| **Vendor trash** | Grey items. Sell every one. |
| **AH** | Auction house. Faction capitals have one; neutral cities like Booty Bay and Gadgetzan serve both sides. |
| **BiS** | Best in slot: the single best item for a gear slot. Endgame talk. |
| **Twink** | A character parked at a PvP bracket cap (19, 29, 39) with the best possible gear. |
| **Enchant** | A permanent stat bonus applied to gear by an enchanter. |

## Groups and Dungeons

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **The trinity** | Tank (holds aggro), Healer (keeps everyone alive), DPS (kills things). |
| **Tank** | The player who gets hit so nobody else has to. Their pace is the group's pace. |
| **Healer** | Keeps the tank and group alive. Watch their Mana bar before pulls. |
| **Need / Greed / Pass** | Loot rolling. Need: equip it now. Greed: sell it. Pass: no interest. When unsure, ask the group. |
| **Ninja looting** | Breaking roll rules. The fastest way to get blacklisted on a server. |
| **PUG** | Pick-up group: random strangers. Be friendly, punctual, and follow the tank. |
| **LFG / LFM** | Looking For Group / Looking For More. How dungeon groups assemble in chat. |
| **Main / Alt** | Primary character / side characters. |
| **Spec** | The talent build. Leveling spec and raid spec are different conversations. |
| **OOM** | Out of mana. The healer's way of saying wait. |
| **Attunement** | A quest chain that unlocks a raid entrance. Start them early — see [Endgame](/docs/endgame). |

## World and Travel

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **FP** | Flight point. Take every one passed. |
| **Hearth / Hearthstone** | Teleport home. Wherever home is. |
| **Bind** | Where the hearthstone is set. Bind at the busiest hub of the current bracket. |
| **Instance** | A dungeon: a private copy for the group. |
| **Raid** | Larger-group content for 10, 20, or 40 players. The endgame. |
| **Contested zone** | Territory both factions share. PvP is possible; on Normal rulesets flagging is opt-in, and some players opt in aggressively. |
| **Gank / Camp** | A higher-level player killing you / killing you repeatedly at your corpse. Abusive camping is reportable; otherwise take the spirit healer and move on. |
| **Battleground bracket** | PvP matches grouped by level range (10–19, 20–29, and so on). |

## Social

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ding** | A level-up. Tradition: announce it, receive grats. |
| **Grats** | Congratulations. Say it when others ding or win loot. |
| **kek** | Horde-side "lol". Born from the Orcish language filter. |
| **PST** | Please send tell: reply by whisper. |
| **Tell / Whisper** | Private message: `/w name message`. |
| **Barrens chat** | The zone-wide chat of the Barrens, famous for chaos and debates that outlive expansions. |
| **Mankrik's wife** | A Crossroads quest target lost somewhere south. So many players asked where she was that she became the game's most famous meme. |
| **DKP / Loot council / EPGP** | Guild systems for handing out raid loot: points per raid, officer decisions, or a points variant. Ask before joining. |

## Money

- 100 copper = 1 silver. 100 silver = 1 gold.
- Repairs consume durability and gold.
- Two landmarks: the **level 40 riding mount** at roughly 100 gold total, and the **level 60 epic mount** at roughly 1000 gold.
- Method: sell everything grey, auction every green that cannot be used. The mount funds itself.

## Death

Dying costs gear durability and a corpse run as a ghost. That is the cheap option. The spirit healer adds minutes of weakness and a bigger repair bill. Corpse run — always. Dying to a bad pull is tuition; rez sickness is a fine.

## Groups and Loot Rules

Dungeons are five players. A classic-style game has no automatic queue: travel to the entrance or get summoned. The etiquette:

1. The tank pulls; the healer's Mana gates the pace.
2. Roll Need only on gear equipped immediately; Greed on sellables; Pass otherwise.
3. When a blue drops and the right choice is unclear, ask the group before rolling.

## The Auction House

The faction capital auction house is where gold compounds. Sell green drops that cannot be used; buy bags and weapons cheaper than vendors sell them. Deposits are small.

Two warnings:

- Never buy gold from websites — it funds bots and gets accounts banned.
- Read every trade window and COD mail before accepting. Scams target new players specifically.

## Group Etiquette

- Roll Need only on gear equipped immediately. Everything else is Greed or Pass.
- Never pull before the tank. The pull belongs to the tank.
- Watch the healer's Mana. A healer at zero means the group stands still.
- Say hi when joining a group, say thanks when leaving. People re-invite the pleasant ones.
- Give grats on dings and loot. It costs nothing and buys everything.
- Do not beg for gold, bags, or boosts. Ask the guild once, politely, and accept no.
- If a wipe was your fault, say so. Owning it is respected; silence is not.

## Legends You Will Encounter

| Legend | The story |
| --- | --- |
| **Leeroy Jenkins** | A paladin who charged in before the plan finished, wiping his raid mid-strategy. Now the battle cry of every doomed idea. |
| **More dots** | From a famous raid-leader meltdown recording. If someone quotes it, they have been playing a long time. |
| **The elevator boss** | The lifts in Thunder Bluff and the Undercity kill more players than most dungeon bosses. Step carefully. |
| **Thunderfury spam** | Linking a famous legendary sword in trade chat as copypasta. Visible within the first week in any city. |
| **Barrens chat** | Where a quest question becomes a three-hour philosophy seminar. Mute it or love it; do not fight it. |

## Guilds

A guild is a server family: organized dungeon runs, help with quests and professions, raid slots at 60, and people who notice when you log in. Guilds distribute raid loot through DKP (points earned per raid, spent on items), loot council (officers decide), or EPGP (a points variant). Ask how a guild handles loot **before** joining. The right guild is worth more than any epic.

## Account Safety

- Whispers offering cheap gold, level boosts, or "account verification" are scams or bots. Report, ignore, never click.
- Never buy gold. It funds bots, wrecks the economy, and gets accounts permanently banned.
- Before accepting any trade or COD mail, hover over every item. Counterfeit trades count on unread items.
- An account is worth more than any character. Use an authenticator if offered. Never share login details — even with friends.
